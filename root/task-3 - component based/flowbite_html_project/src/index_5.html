community guidelines

Keeping the show on the road

KURIO is your trusted space for sharing and discovering advice. To keep it meaningful, we ask all members to follow these simple principles:

1. Keep it real

Share advice and experiences that are genuine and grounded in what you know. KURIO thrives on authenticity.

2. Stay kind

This is a space built on trust and respect. Support others as you’d want to be supported.

3. Privacy first

What’s shared in KURIO stays in KURIO. Protect your own and others’ privacy at all times.

4. No noise, no clutter

Stick to concise, helpful answers. This isn’t the place for self-promotion, spam, or off-topic chatter.

5. Bring your best

Every recommendation should feel considered—KURIO is about quality over quantity.
